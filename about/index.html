VPA Tour

About VPA

VPA (Vietnam Pickleball Asia) is a pickleball tournament series from Đà Nẵng. Our flagship event is Papi Point Finals.

VPA is an independent event organiser — not an association or federation.

How it works for players

  1. Find an event on the Events page.
  2. Register in a minute with the rating you already have (DUPR, PVNA, VDPR…).
  3. Play. Follow your games and live scores on the event page.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

Using VPA is free. Some events have an entry fee, which you pay straight to the organiser.

I don't have a rating. Can I play?

Yes. Pick "Self-rated" and give your best guess — the organiser will check it with you.

What is the "VPA level"?

A single number we work out from the ratings you give us, so players from different rating apps can be put in fair groups. It is in beta; your own rating always comes first.

Where do the rankings come from?

From ratings that are already public on DUPR, PVNA, VDPR and other systems. To fix or remove your entry, use the privacy page.

For organisers

Free tools to run your own event: player list, round robin or knockout draw, court-by-court scores and live standings.
