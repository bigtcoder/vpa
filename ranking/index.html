VPA Vietnam Ranking (estimate)

Covers players listed on Vietnam's PVNA, VDPR, PICKNA (Nghệ An) and LOOL systems and public DUPR leaderboards (apps without public listings — VNPA, VPTA, LMPVN, Sport Connect — are not included yet) — not a global ranking. Every rating is shown as a VPA level (DUPR-equivalent doubles) and ranked by a conservative score (estimate − 1 SD) so uncertain estimates don't jump the queue. It will firm up as VPA match results are logged.
