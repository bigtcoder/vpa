How does this ranking work?

It covers players listed publicly on PVNA, VDPR, PICKNA (Nghệ An), LOOL and DUPR leaderboards. It is not a world ranking. Players are ordered by their strongest rating; the VPA score tab uses a careful estimate so players with little data don't jump ahead.

Names, ratings and cities shown here are already public on those systems. To fix or remove your entry, use the privacy page.
